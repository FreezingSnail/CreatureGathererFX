Huskfield Meadow

Seed weevils share ribbed pods and grain stores with wind tufts and ScrambleSnail. Tall stalks shelter a worn dirt harvest lane; loose husks and seed fluff gather at the field edges.

Scenery motifs: Ribbed husks, grain heads, windseed fluff and a small granary.

Creature references use 2× battle-sprite scale; the world player remains 16×16.

Dewcap Grove

DewBud and NectarBloom favor the wet edge, while Sporeling, SporeKeeper and MossMole occupy the shaded logs. Skimray connects the plant theme to the pools. A leaf-litter path threads between mushroom groves.

Scenery motifs: Dew leaves, nectar blooms, spotted caps, mossy logs and shallow pools.

Creature references use 2× battle-sprite scale; the world player remains 16×16.

Shellstone Tide

SkitterCrab, Conelet and Horseshoe inhabit the shell banks; SiltSkorp and Platefin suit the silt pools. SpireSquid gives the old spiral fossils an elder connection. Textured shell sand separates the exposed fossil beds from the water.

Scenery motifs: Spiral fossils, armored shells, tidal pools, layered reef stone and a short jetty.

Creature references use 2× battle-sprite scale; the world player remains 16×16.

Cinderflint Quarry

CoalGrub, CinderCocoon and FurnaceBeetle form the hot-soil family. Ember and WaggleWorm fit the vents, while Flintling and GeodeGuard inhabit the cooler mineral face. Packed grit lanes connect the coal working to the crystal pocket.

Scenery motifs: Coal, clinker, vents, faceted geodes and abandoned mine timbers.

Creature references use 2× battle-sprite scale; the world player remains 16×16.
