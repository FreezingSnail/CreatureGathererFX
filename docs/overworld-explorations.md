# Overworld visual explorations

[Open the combined viewer](overworld-explorations.html) to compare both
rounds on one page.

The newest [fresh pass](overworld-explorations.html#fresh-round) appears
first: a 295-tile kit with mountain pass, marsh, ancient ruins, snowy
highlands and volcanic badlands, followed by modern towers, coast,
islands, desert, oasis and the original four areas. Fifteen camera studies
come from a layered 96×60 world in three art directions and three dithering amounts. Buildings are
assembled from transparent 16×16 pieces; repeated floor rows create short
and tall versions of the same towers.

The fresh gallery shows all nine combinations at once for every area:
Soft blocks, Ink silhouettes and Stepped etching in columns, with clean,
light and heavy textures in rows. The world and atlas have separate style
and texture selectors below the gallery.

The earlier scene-led concepts and 64-tile second round follow on the same
page. Each pass retains its own controls and source material while sharing
the same 128×64 viewport and 16×16 player scale.

Individual pages remain available for focused review: [round 1](overworld-identity-spike.html)
and [round 2](overworld-tileset-round2.html).
