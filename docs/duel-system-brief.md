# RPS duel battle system — epic brief

Status: planning input, 2026-10-15. Owner direction: replace the current Pokemon/SMT-style battle
with a hidden-simultaneous-pick duel over a small type web. This brief carries every rule and number
validated in the worked example (`mock/`) so Sol can do the in-depth plan without re-deriving it.
No production source changes are authorized by this document; implementation children are deferred
until the planning review lands.

## Why now

- The current battle is 8 types with dual typing, PP, stat stages, statuses, effects, accuracy and
  staged playback. On 128x64 1bpp there is no room to communicate any of that; players must
  memorize a 64-cell chart to make one decision.
- The worked example proved the alternative is *communicable and playable*: 5 types (10 beats facts),
  one flat damage table, arrows/↑/↓ on the picks, badges on the sprites, x/y table on the page.
- Current shipping image is over the flash limit (control reproduced at 29,790 B vs 29,696 B limit in
  `docs/battle-flash-audit.md`), and the battle family is 17,914 B of that. The duel deletes exactly
  the machinery that is expensive to keep: damage formula, effects, end-turn ticks, status gates,
  turn ordering, PP, and most menu/presenter staging.

## The system as validated in the mock

Types (5, folded at build time):
`spirit > fire`, `water > spirit`, `water > earth`, `water > fire`, `earth > spirit`, `earth > fire`,
`earth > plant`, `fire > plant`, `plant > spirit`, `plant > water` — a full tournament, 10 edges.
Fold map from the authored 8: lightning→fire, elder→spirit, wind→water; fire/water/earth/plant stay.
The same solver that guarantees set coverage lives in `tools/battle-matchup-solver.mjs`; the 5-type
table is `mock/matrices.js` (`matrices[1]`, id "5"), generated by `tools/battle-matrices-spike.mjs`.

Round loop:
1. Both sides pick hidden: one attack (one of the active creature's own attack moves, status/utility
   moves excluded) or a switch (swap cell).
2. Reveal after 550 ms.
3. Attack vs attack: the pick types decide win/lose/neutral (chart above). Winner deals; loser chips;
   neutral/mirror trades.
4. Damage: **win 14, chip 4, trade 6** (each side's number scales by its own creature-type matchup:
   ×1.5 when the move type beats the opposing creature's type, ×0.5 when it loses to it; ×1 otherwise).
5. Switch: the side that did not switch lands a **default win (14, × matchup modifier vs the incoming
   creature's type)**. Both switching deals no damage.
6. KO: fainting the active promotes the first living member; a KO on an incoming swap reverts to the
   outgoing creature. A side loses only when all three members have fainted. Draw when both do.
7. No power, no PP, no accuracy, no stages, no statuses, no effects in this system.

Teams: three creatures per side. Demo rolls random 3v3 from the seed, or starts from authored sample
teams (`mock/teams.js`); preset members carry **retyped types** that drive badges and matchup
modifiers while the creature keeps its own moves/stats/HP.

UI facts (all implemented and reviewed in the mock):
- Footer pick row: one cell per attack move (type icon, ↑/↓ marker in the bonus spelling) plus a
  `swap` cell; white box = cursor.
- Centre strip: `R#` round, selected move type icon, power digits only in the power spelling
  (the duel has no power), `swap` text when the swap cell is selected.
- HP rows: numbers + bar per side plus **team pips** (filled = alive, underline = active).
- Type badges: white-framed chips on each sprite (foe bottom-left, you bottom-right) showing the
  folded/retyped types.
- Feedback lines: `BONUS WIN/LOSE` (×1.5), `WEAK WIN/LOSE` (×0.5), `RPS WIN/LOSE`, `TRADE`,
  `DEFAULT WIN`, `SWAP`, `TIE` (stances).

AI (personas, reused across groups of trainers): `random`, `reader` (counters your last revealed
type), `frequency` (your most-used), `anti-repeat` (discounts your last pick), `exploiter` (modal type
of your picks after it read you — challenge areas only). Read-rate is the chance it acts on its model;
fallback when no counter exists is uniform random; it switches only when its active kit has no counter
and a living member does. `AI READ` is a debug tell. `tools/battle-duel-spike.mjs` measured the
read-rate curves: reader vs random player 58%→33% player wins as rate rises; a fully reading `reader`
is exploited by predictable rotation (62%→100% player wins) — keep persona defaults below ~75% until
personas mix fallbacks.

## Assets

Existing and reusable:
- Type icon art for all five types (`mock/icons.js`): spirit, water, earth, fire, plant (+ status/none).
- 5-type folded table (`mock/matrices.js` id "5"): order, edges, chart, stats.
- Authored sample teams (`mock/teams.js`): balanced, anchors, support, retyped, trap.
- Fold map (8→5) in `tools/battle-matrices-spike.mjs` (`FOLDS[5]`).

New assets the real implementation will need:
- Packed 5×5 chart data for fxdata (10 edge pairs, or a 25-cell 2-bit nibble table; decide in planning).
- Build-time fold/retype map emitted from authoring data (no runtime folding on AVR).
- Authored beta teams (three creatures + types) for trainers/encounters, replacing the demo presets.
- Bitmap captions for the new feedback vocabulary (`BONUS WIN`, `WEAK LOSE`, `DEFAULT WIN`, `SWAP`,
  `RPS WIN/LOSE`, `TRADE`) appended to the existing authored text source, never hand-edited.
- Optional: a fourth/fifth icon variant if the fold changes; current art already covers the set.

## Complexity and flash estimate (estimation, not measurement)

From `docs/battle-flash-audit.md` (whole-image removal sensitivity; rows do NOT sum because of LTO):

| deletable with the duel | measured |
| --- | ---: |
| `computeDamage` core + modifier/stage/class rows | 776 + 22 + 78 + 38 B |
| effects application / end-turn ticks / status gates | 662 + 434 + 240 B |
| priority + speed ordering | 278 B |
| PP spend/exhaustion (+menu checks) | 84 B+ |
| `applySwitch` rebuild + party setup (kept partially) | 1,194 + 580 B |
| menu router + drawing | 1,392 + 990 B |
| presenter drawing | 1,152 B |

Kept regardless: scene/HUD sprites and bars (942 B) plus new badges/pips (~+50–150 B); encounter
dispatch, FX transition reads, HP sync shell. New duel logic (hidden pick, reveal, default win,
personas, history) estimated +0.6–1.0 KB. Rough expectation: **−3.5…−5.5 KB flash vs the current
battle family, static RAM down too** (duel state ≈ 30–40 B/side vs `Combatant` copies with
`Move moves[4]`, status and stage bytes). LTO overlap means the sum is not additive; a whole-image A/B
build is mandatory before any number is credited.

## Proposed spike plan (for Sol to detail and sequence)

1. Compile-time `CGFX_DUEL_BATTLE` behind the existing Flow/session shell; port chart + flat resolver
   + personas; keep the current menu/scene temporarily. Measure whole image vs control.
2. Replace menu with the compact pick row + single-phase feedback; add badges/pips; measure.
3. Delete the now-unreachable damage/effects/turn/status/PP paths; measure; verify FX-read counters
   (transition-only metadata reads) and stack headroom with `test_stack`.
4. Port authored teams + fold map into fxdata; balance pass with the simulator; device suites.

## Open questions for the in-depth plan

1. Resolution constants: 14/4/6 with ×1.5/×0.5 — confirm or tune (does a bonus win shorten fights too
   much at typical HP?).
2. Switch targeting UI for 3-member teams (next living vs a picker row); both switching = no damage
   or both swap for free?
3. Fold map finality (lightning→fire, elder→spirit, wind→water) and whether dual-typed creatures keep
   two folded types for the matchup lookup.
4. Persona set per trainer archetype and default read rates; whether `exploiter` ships at all.
5. Status/utility moves: excluded in the spike — do items/gathering keep any non-attack interaction?
6. Whether the stances spelling (4-type cycle, universal stances) is kept only as a demo or has a
   gameplay role.
7. Caption/asset list and where the new strings enter generated text data.
8. Save/party contract: teams are 3 members per side; confirm party size, storage changes and HP sync
   from the current `Player::creatureHPs` contract.
9. Measurement discipline: one build lane, paired whole-image builds, evidence retention per
   `docs/dev-flow.md` and the storage rules.

## Non-goals

- No adoption into shipping firmware in this epic without the planning review and measured spikes.
- Do not remove gathering/escape/encounter systems; only their battle-interaction routes change.
- Do not regenerate or hand-edit generated artifacts outside `make gen`.
