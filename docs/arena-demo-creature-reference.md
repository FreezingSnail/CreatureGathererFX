## Type effectiveness

This chart shows the type matchup for a damaging move against a defender with one type. For dual-type creatures, combine the two defending-type modifiers. Same-type attacks also receive a 2× bonus. Status moves do not deal type-based damage.

| Attack ↓ / Defense → | Spirit | Water | Wind | Earth | Fire | Lightning | Plant | Elder |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Spirit | 1× | 1× | 1× | 1× | 1× | 1× | 1× | 0× |
| Water | 1× | 1× | 2× | ½× | 2× | 1× | ½× | ½× |
| Wind | 1× | 1× | 1× | 2× | ½× | ½× | 2× | ½× |
| Earth | 1× | 2× | ½× | 1× | 1× | 2× | ½× | ½× |
| Fire | 1× | 0× | 2× | ½× | 1× | 2× | 2× | 1× |
| Lightning | 1× | 2× | 2× | ½× | 1× | 2× | 2× | 1× |
| Plant | 1× | 0× | 2× | ½× | 1× | 2× | 2× | 1× |
| Elder | 1× | 0× | 2× | ½× | 1× | 2× | 2× | 1× |

`0×` means immune; `½×` means half damage; `2×` means double damage. When the two defensive types combine, the modifiers multiply (for example, 2× and ½× produce neutral damage).
