At the largest clamped stage numerator, `7,905 * 6 = 47,430`, so the multiply fits 16 bits and the current `> 0xffff` saturation is unreachable for game callers. Spike A replaces the 32-bit intermediate with 16-bit arithmetic and verifies exact stage/damage outputs; spike B replaces table lookup by the equivalent clamped ratios `2/(2-stage)` for negative stages and `(2+stage)/2` otherwise. The latter is a separate compile hypothesis because division/code layout may offset the 18-byte SRAM removal. **Risk: low-medium** for truncation order and extreme stage values. Compare all 9 stages, clamp endpoints, every 8-bit speed, max 31×255 attack, low defense/immunity, 255 damage saturation, AI speed utility; measure shipping flash/static and stack. This is distinct from the already adopted staged-*speed* change.
