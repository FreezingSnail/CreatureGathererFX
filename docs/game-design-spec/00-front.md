<h1>CreatureGathererFX — Game Design Spec</h1>
<p class="sub">Creature-collecting demake for the <strong>Arduboy FX</strong> (ATmega32u4), rendered in native 1bpp at 52 fps. A compact overworld where you gather lures, trigger wild creature duels, win or capture creatures, grow them by experience and evolution, and keep a 64-slot persistent collection on the cartridge.</p>
