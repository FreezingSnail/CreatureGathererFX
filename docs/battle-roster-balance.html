Balance pass · four role-focused move slots

GeodeGuard gains Ironbody for setup durability; StaticMite gets Coil for a boost to later lightning attacks; ShardWisp adds Smite as stronger spirit coverage; SaplingSage gains Rejuvenate as a healer option. Pairwise and team simulations found clear gains for StaticMite and ShardWisp, a small team-rate lift for GeodeGuard, and no measurable win-rate change for SaplingSage. The healer role is intentional; simulator win rates are screening evidence, not human win-rate predictions.
