mode what it tests
A · current shipping 8-type dual-type menu: nothing tells you the matchup. Memorize-to-play baseline.
B · trim 4-type single-type cycle (flame > bloom > tide > stone > flame) with ▲/▼/·/× on every move row, optional foe HP numbers, optional press-turn (super hit acts again).
C · duel simultaneous hidden stance pick, 550 ms reveal, damage per clash; 3- or 4-stance cycle, affinity stance ×1.5, AI counters your last pick at the read rate.
readout meaning
center column FOE row: label or exact HP (trim) + foe type icons; bar. YOU row: HP numbers + bar. Move detail under YOU: type icon, power, remaining uses (* = unlimited).
▲ ▼ · × final damage modifier for that move including STAB (the number you would actually deal).
EXTRA TURN press-turn fired: the same move acts again this turn (both sides can trigger it).
AFF / R3 duel: your affinity stance, and the current round.

Data, damage, HP and move-use limits are generated/ported from the shipping data and C++ (Damage.cpp, Type.hpp, MoveUses.hpp) at stat stages 0. Not simulated: statuses, stages, effects, accuracy, party/switching, escape/gather, animations. Foe HP is hidden in mode A like production. Data: node tools/battle-design-spike-data.mjs · tests: node --test mock/battle-mock.test.js.
