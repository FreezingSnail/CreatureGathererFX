Type-count tables — is 8 too many to memorize?

Each table folds the authored 8 types into fewer and solves a triangle web against the folded creature kits (node tools/battle-matrices-spike.mjs). Memorization load is the edge count: every edge is one "beats" fact. Pick the count that stays learnable, then choose that table in any duel window's types selector to play it.
