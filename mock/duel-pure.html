Pure RPS: both sides pick one of their creature's moves, hidden, and only the type relation decides — move power, stats and affinity are ignored. Win deals a flat 14, the loser chips 4 back, trade hits both for 6. Same 8-type web as the power duel (25 edges, 16 triangles); the highlighted row/column follow the setup creatures. Compare with the power window to see whether numbers add anything or just noise.

Centre strip: R# = round, icon = the selected move's type, number = move power (0 = utility move). No power number is shown in this spelling; the result lines read RPS WIN / RPS LOSE / TRADE.

Opponent AI: personas (reader / frequency / anti-repeat / exploiter, plus random) are reused across trainer groups; pick yours in the controls. With no counter against its guess it picks uniformly at random — switching creatures is the real-game answer for that spot and is not in this 1v1 prototype. Switching: the swap cell (or a full bench with no counter) forfeits the round — the other side lands a default win on the incoming creature (14, or 21 in the bonus spelling when their move beats the arrival). Parties are two creatures per side; the bench is the next roster entry in this prototype.
