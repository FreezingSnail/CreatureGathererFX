Matchup table (row attacks column)

Both sides pick one of their own creature's moves, hidden, then reveal: the 8-type web decides. Win = full hit (your move power, ×1.5 when the move type is your creature's type), lose = blocked plus chip, neutral or mirror = trade chip. Type badges on each sprite show both creatures' types (white-framed chips); the footer row shows your creature's equipped moves; ✱ marks an affinity pick. Twin windows: E · pure RPS (no power) and F · bonus (x1.5 when your move type beats the opposing creature's type). Status/utility moves are excluded from this spike, so 17 creatures field fewer than three attack moves (bell and SquibbleSnail carry one each) — three-move kits are an authoring pass. The AI counters your previous type at the read rate when it has a counter.

Centre strip: R# = round, icon = the selected move's type, number = move power (0 = utility move). ✱ appears on the cell of an affinity pick (×1.5).

Opponent AI: personas (reader / frequency / anti-repeat / exploiter, plus random) are reused across trainer groups; pick yours in the controls. With no counter against its guess it picks uniformly at random — switching creatures is the real-game answer for that spot and is not in this 1v1 prototype. Teams are 3v3, rolled randomly from the seed when the battle starts (reroll the seed for new teams). Switching forfeits the round: the swap cell sends out the next living member and the other side lands a default win on the arrival (14, or 21 in the bonus spelling when their move beats it). The AI switches only when its active kit holds no counter for its guess and a living member does.
