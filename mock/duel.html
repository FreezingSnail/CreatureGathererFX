Both sides pick one of their own creature's moves, hidden, then reveal: the 8-type wheel decides. Win = full hit (your move power, ×1.5 when the move type is your creature's type), lose = blocked plus chip, neutral or mirror = trade chip. Wheel: wind > earth > lightning > water > fire > plant > spirit > elder > wind — each type beats the next and loses to the previous; everything else is neutral. The footer row shows your creature's equipped moves as type icons; AFF marks an affinity pick. The AI counters your previous type at the read rate.
